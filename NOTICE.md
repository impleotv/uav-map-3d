# Asset notices

The four glTF platform presets are original Impleo artwork, transferred from
STView with their generator in `scripts/generate_scene_models.py`.

The EGM96 15-minute geoid dataset is public domain; source, attribution and
SHA-256 are retained in `assets/geoid/NOTICE.txt`.

Cesium is an external dependency. Its LICENSE.md, ThirdParty.json and
ThirdParty.extra.json accompany the runtime resources staged by the Vite plugin.
Preserve data-provider and model attribution when supplying external resources.
